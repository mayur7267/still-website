The whole idea

Two things you can do with a thought.

Every other journal assumes you want to keep everything forever. Still doesn't. Some things you write to remember. Some things you write only to get them out of your head — and those should be allowed to leave.

Keep it.

Star it, and it stays. Search it, revisit it, watch it turn into a record of who you were.

Burn it.

Watch it go up in flames and it's gone for good — not moved to a folder, not sitting on a server, not recoverable by anyone. The relief is the point.
